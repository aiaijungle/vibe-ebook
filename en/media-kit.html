Short introduction

AI Bible is a five-volume practical series for people who want to turn AI ideas into useful work: marketing drafts, small digital tools, agent workflows, verified code changes and business operations.

From your first reviewed draft to agent operations, AI Bible connects learning with concrete outputs.
