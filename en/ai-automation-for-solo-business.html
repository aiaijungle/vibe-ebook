AI automation starts with records, not tools.

For a solo business, automation works when every lead, buyer, content asset, and next action is captured. Then AI and tools can help you follow up consistently.

The 6-part automation loop

1. Create content from one clear idea.
2. Send readers to a landing page or free PDF.
3. Capture the lead with email and source.
4. Tag the lead: free PDF, buyer, diagnosis, B2B.
5. Trigger the next action: thank-you, review request, offer, or alert.
6. Record everything in Notion, CSV, or a database.
