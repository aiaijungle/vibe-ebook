AI orchestration is the skill of conducting a team of AI.

Beyond asking one agent a question, real automation begins when you wire multiple agents and tools (via MCP) into one workflow.

See the system in the AI Bible

What is AI orchestration?

It is conducting multiple AI agents as a single workflow. Split roles into Planner, Worker, Reviewer, and Orchestrator and you get a repeatable work loop instead of one-off answers.

What is MCP, and why "SEO for the AI era"?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) connects AI directly to your tools and data. As you optimized for search with SEO, you connect to AI with MCP so it finds and uses your service - linking Notion, Figma, and GitHub.

Can a non-developer do this?

Yes. It is delegation design - roles and approval rules - not coding. The AI Bible shows how a non-coder built a 30-agent system running 24/7.
