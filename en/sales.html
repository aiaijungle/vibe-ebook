Where should I start?

Start with Volume 1 for a reviewed content workflow, Volume 2 for a small tool, Volume 3 for agent coordination, Volume 4 for Claude Code practice, or Volume 5 for operations and ontology.

English web readers are available for all five volumes. The new English companion guides are linked above, with public introductions and password access to the complete practice material. Individual tool screenshots or legacy examples may retain their original language.

How does reader access work?

Readers use the existing purchase and unlock flow. Check the checkout page for current terms. For purchase or access help, contact housepeoplekorea@gmail.com.

Can my team use this in a workshop?

Contact us about the audience, intended use and delivery language. Workshop scope and any licensing arrangements must be agreed separately.
