AI Bible Global / Prompt to Workflow

Short answer

A prompt becomes valuable when it becomes a workflow.

A good prompt creates an answer. A good workflow creates repeatable output, saves the result, and triggers the next business action.

Read the system Download free PDF

The formula

You are [role].

Your goal is to create [output].

Use [source material].

Follow [quality criteria].

Review your own output, revise it, and suggest 3 next actions.

Workflow version

After the output is created, save it in a record, tag the status, decide the next action, and connect it to a publishing, email, or checkout flow.
